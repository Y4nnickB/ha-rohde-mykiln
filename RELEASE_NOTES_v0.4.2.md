## What's new in 0.4.2

ROHDE has replied to our request: the ROHDE and myKiln names and the ROHDE logo may be used to identify the products and service this integration works with. This release applies that. No change in behaviour.

### Changed
- The integration **icon** is now the myKiln app icon and the **logo** is the ROHDE logo, with a white variant for dark mode (shown by Home Assistant 2026.3 or newer).

### Documentation
- New page **Statement from ROHDE** (`docs/rohde-statement.md`).
- Updated disclaimer: myKiln is a service of Helmut ROHDE GmbH; this integration is an independent project and is **not developed or officially supported by ROHDE**. Please report problems here, not to ROHDE support.

### Removed
- The original kiln artwork and `tools/make_brand.py`.

### Upgrading
Update through HACS and restart Home Assistant. No reconfiguration is needed.

**Full changelog:** see `CHANGELOG.md`.
