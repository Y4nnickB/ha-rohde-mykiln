## What's new in 0.4.1

Small maintenance release. No change in behaviour.

### Fixed
- The **documentation** and **issue tracker** links of the integration (shown in Home Assistant and HACS) now point to this repository.

### Repository
- GitHub Actions now validate every change with **hassfest** and **HACS**.
- Issue forms for **bug reports** and for **"Tested with my kiln"** reports. Reports from other controllers (ST 411 / 630 / 632) and from kiln states that have not been verified yet (idle, heating, holding, delayed start) are very welcome.

### Upgrading
Update through HACS and restart Home Assistant. No reconfiguration is needed.

**Full changelog:** see `CHANGELOG.md`.
